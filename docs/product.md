# AXIOM Product Model

AXIOM Launcher is the core paid product: a proprietary Android home environment planned for sale at `$4.99`.

VOID.SYS is the intended/default AXIOM icon system. It is proprietary, integrated natively by package `com.outervoid.voidsys`, and also planned as a standalone `$1.99` icon pack.

VOID.GAME / Axiom Shooter is a protected hidden module. It is launched through AXIOM after a long press on the AXIOM logo/name and is not positioned as a standalone product.

Product positioning: **A controlled Android environment, not just a launcher.**
